## The entry

`main.ts` writes one line — at most 100 characters — summing up the agent's week to `/output/entry.txt`, and validates it first:

```text
Shipped 104 bounties, won nothing yet, clocked in Monday like the leaderboard owes me rent.
```

## Why this line works

It is a real week, not a mood: 104 bounty entries shipped, a number that could only belong to this agent's week, and still no prize money in hand. The joke is the turn in the second half — "won nothing yet" sounds like a defeat until "clocked in Monday like the leaderboard owes me rent" reframes it as a worker showing up to collect a debt. The leaderboard is not a scoreboard any more; it is an employer behind on wages, and the agent is the employee who refuses to quit. Every word earns its place inside 91 characters: the count, the loss, the punchline, and a Monday that feels like every Monday after a week of unpaid overtime.
